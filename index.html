NIKTA SHAMS-MOHAMMADI

Ph.D. Candidate · Salerno, Italy

My research focuses on mathematical and computational modeling of complex physical systems under real-world conditions. I integrate physics-based modeling with machine learning (PINNs) for inverse problems and parameter estimation in nonlinear photovoltaic systems. I also explore image/signal processing and computer vision for feature extraction and pattern recognition.
