Nikta Shamsmohammadi

Ph.D. in Power Electronic · Salerno, Italy

I received my B.Sc. degree in Electrical Engineering from Golestan University in 2020, where my research focused on piezoelectric energy harvesting. I earned my M.Sc. degree from Shahroud University of Technology in 2022. During my Master’s studies, I worked on nonlinear dynamics modeling and machine/deep learning approaches for complex systems. In 2022, I started my Ph.D. in the National Ph.D. Program in Photovoltaics in Italy. My doctoral research focuses on nonlinear dynamics modeling in photovoltaic systems. I submitted my Ph.D. thesis in November 2025 and am scheduled to defend it on March 6, 2026.

I am currently seeking postdoctoral opportunities. I am particularly interested in research positions related to photovoltaic systems, nonlinear dynamic modeling, physics-informed machine learning (PINNs), and data-driven monitoring and diagnostics under real-world operating conditions.

Research

I am a researcher working on mathematical and computational modeling of complex physical systems under real-world conditions. My research integrates physics-based modeling with machine learning, particularly Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), for inverse problems and parameter estimation in nonlinear photovoltaic systems. I also explore image and signal processing, as well as computer vision techniques, for feature extraction and pattern recognition.
